07 · niyam

What protects the plan

🚦

Never skip a milestone

A phase starts only when the previous one has proven its numbers.

🏦

Savings never fund the farm

Expansion is paid by farm and gur income, apart from small starting costs.

🛣️

Never sell the highway land

It's the most valuable long-term asset and the future outlet.

📝

Everything in writing

Roles with father, rent to cousins, contractor terms, borewell and power costs.

🚫

No loans, no show-off

No car EMI, no city flat, no ₹25L wedding. These restart the race.

🤝

Share it with your partner

The person you marry needs to want this life too.
